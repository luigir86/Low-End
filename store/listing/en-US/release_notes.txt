Harmony's progressions now last 4, 8 or 12 bars, as musical phrases do: the II–V–I holds its I for two bars, so the loop starts again where you expect it. New: the circle of fifths of Autumn Leaves, the minor blues, I–IV–V–IV and an eight-bar I–VI–IV–V as in Stand by Me, with new example songs. A chord also writes its notes out on the dial, and the keyboard for typing a number opens only when you touch it.
