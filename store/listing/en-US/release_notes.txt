Harmony's progressions now last 4, 8 or 12 bars, as musical phrases do: the II–V–I holds its I for two bars, so the loop starts again where you expect it. The menu goes from the easiest to the richest, in sections for pop and rock, blues and jazz, with new ones: the circle of fifths of Autumn Leaves, the minor blues and I–IV–V–IV. A chord also writes its notes out on the dial.
