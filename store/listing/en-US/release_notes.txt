A new piano for Harmony: a grand piano recorded in stereo, that rings and dies away as under a pianist's hands. Clearer sound, in time with the light also over Bluetooth, and it stops when you unplug the headphones. The tuner has an instrument menu (guitar, violin, viola, cello, ukulele, bass). Tidier rows under the dials, a notification that goes away when the sound stops, and every setting can be changed by an assistant.
