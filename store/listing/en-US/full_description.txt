Low End is a practice companion for musicians: a metronome, a piano harmony section and a tuner in one light app. Made by an amateur double bass player with the friends from the orchestra in mind, it is useful to everyone. It is free, has no ads, needs no account and works fully offline.

METRONOME
• Tempo from 30 to 250 BPM: tap the number to type it, hold − or + to move in steps, or use TAP.
• Time signatures 1/1, 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8, 7/8, 9/8 and 12/8, with the beats grouped the way you count them.
• A ring that lights up beat by beat, with the downbeat marked, and a short, dry click that stays steady, straight or swing. The piano follows the swing too.

HARMONY
• Twelve keys on a dial: touch a note and it plays on a real piano, in time with the metronome.
• The note alone, or a major, minor or seventh chord, struck once a bar, on every beat or as an arpeggio.
• Progressions to practise over, one chord per bar: II–V–I (major and minor), I–IV–V, I–VI–IV–V and the twelve-bar blues, in any key, each named after a song you know.
• Choose the octave of the root and how the chord is laid out. Set the balance between metronome and harmony with one slider.

TUNER
• Chromatic, with a round strobe and the note in the middle: play any note and it shows up, no string to choose first.
• Hears low and high notes alike, from 28 to 700 Hz (a low B on a five-string bass up to a high violin note).
• Reference A: 415, 430, 432 or 438 to 445 Hz. The sound is analysed on the phone and never recorded.

MADE FOR PLAYING
• Swipe left and right to change section. Touch the tab you are on to start or stop its sound; hold another tab to do it from anywhere.
• Keeps playing with the screen off, with a notification that says what is sounding. Optional: keep the screen on.
• A dark look in bronze and ebony, easy on the eyes on stage; English and Italian; note names as C D E or Do Re Mi.
• Built to be accessible: screen-reader labels, enlargeable text, and colours that are never the only cue.

PRIVATE AND OPEN
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no internet connection needed.
• The microphone is used only while the tuner is on screen.
• Free and open-source software under the MIT License: https://github.com/luigir86/Low-End
• Piano sounds from the Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (public domain).
