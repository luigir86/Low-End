Low End is a practice companion for double bass and electric bass players: a metronome, a piano drone and a tuner in one light app. It is free, has no ads, needs no account and works fully offline.

METRONOME
• Tempo from 30 to 250 BPM: tap the number to type it, hold − or + to move in steps, or use TAP.
• Time signatures 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8, 7/8, 9/8 and 12/8, with the beats grouped the way you count them.
• A ring that lights up beat by beat, with the downbeat marked, and a woodblock click that stays steady.

DRONE
• Twelve keys on a dial: touch a note and it plays on a real piano, in time with the metronome.
• The note alone, or a major, minor or seventh chord, struck once a bar, on every beat or as an arpeggio.
• Progressions to practise over, one chord per bar: II–V–I (major and minor), I–IV–V, I–VI–IV–V and the twelve-bar blues, in any key.
• Choose the octave of the root and how the chord is laid out. Set the balance between drone and metronome with one slider.

TUNER
• Chromatic, with a round strobe and the note in the middle: play any note and it shows up, no string to choose first.
• Made for the low register, down to the low B of five-string basses.
• Tunings for orchestral and solo double bass, C extension, five strings, and four-, five- and six-string electric bass, plus drop D.
• Reference A from 438 to 445 Hz. The sound is analysed on the phone and never recorded.

MADE FOR PLAYING
• Swipe left and right to change section. Hold a tab to start or stop its sound from anywhere.
• Keeps playing with the screen off, with a notification that says what is sounding. Optional: keep the screen on.
• Light, dark or automatic theme; English and Italian; note names as C D E or Do Re Mi.
• Built to be accessible: screen-reader labels, enlargeable text, and colours that are never the only cue.

PRIVATE AND OPEN
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no internet connection needed.
• The microphone is used only while the tuner is on screen.
• Free and open-source software under the MIT License: https://github.com/luigir86/Low-End
• Piano sounds from the Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (public domain).
