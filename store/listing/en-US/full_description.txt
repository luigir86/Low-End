Low End is a practice companion for musicians: a metronome, a piano harmony section and a tuner in one light app. Made by an amateur double bass player with the friends from the orchestra in mind, it is useful to everyone. It is free, has no ads, needs no account and works fully offline.

METRONOME
• Tempo from 30 to 250 BPM: type it with the pencil, hold − or + to run through it, or use TAP.
• Time signatures 1/1, 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8, 7/8, 9/8 and 12/8, with the beats grouped the way you count them.
• A ring that lights up beat by beat, with the downbeat marked, and a short, dry click that stays steady. One pill holds the time signature and a menu of what fills each beat (quarters, eighths, triplets, sixteenths, and swung eighths and sixteenths, as MuseScore plays "Swing"); or choose a click on 2 and 4 only.

HARMONY
• Twelve keys on a dial: touch a note and it plays on a real piano, in time with the metronome.
• The note alone, or a major, minor or seventh chord, struck once a bar, on every beat or as an arpeggio.
• Progressions to practise over, one chord per bar: II–V–I (major and minor), I–IV–V, I–VI–IV–V and the twelve-bar blues, in any key, each named after a song you know, with the Roman numerals on the keys.
• Example songs set the key, the progression and the tempo in one tap.
• Or a held tone instead of the piano. The root sits in a middle-low octave that a phone speaker plays well; while it sounds, the notes not heard fade on the dial.
• Start the harmony while the click is running and it comes in on the next bar, with a countdown on the dial.

TUNER
• Chromatic, with a round strobe and the note in the middle: play any note and it shows up, no string to choose first. Or pick guitar, violin, viola, cello, ukulele or bass strings.
• Hears low and high notes alike, from 28 to 2800 Hz (a low B on a five-string bass up to the top of the violin and the flute).
• For B♭, E♭ and F instruments it can name the written note.
• Reference A: every hertz from 415 to 445 (415, 432, 440 and 442 in one tap). The sound is analysed on the phone and never recorded.
• If the metronome or the harmony is playing, touch the tuner's dial and they pause, with a Resume button.

MADE FOR PLAYING
• Each section starts and stops its own sound from the middle of its dial. The tabs and the swipes only change section.
• Swipe left and right to change section; for rehearsals, lock the gestures in the settings.
• Keeps playing with the screen off, with a notification that says what is sounding. The screen can stay on while you play.
• A dark look in bronze and ebony, easy on the eyes on stage; English and Italian; note names as C D E or Do Re Mi.
• Built to be accessible: screen-reader labels, enlargeable text, and colours that are never the only cue.

PRIVATE AND OPEN
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no internet connection needed.
• The microphone is used only while the tuner is on screen.
• Free and open-source software under the MIT License: https://github.com/luigir86/Low-End
• Piano sounds from the Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (public domain).
