# Privacy

Low End collects no personal data.

- **Microphone.** The tuner uses the microphone only while it is listening (after you tap *Start listening*), and stops as soon as you leave the tuner or the app goes to the background. The sound is analysed on the phone to find the note and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
- **No network.** The app does not connect to the internet: no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting.

If this ever changes (for example a future, optional sheet-music conversion service), this page and the app will say so before anything is sent.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/luigir86/Low-End/issues.
